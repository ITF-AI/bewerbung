01 · The systems challenge

26 modules. One shared foundation. AI that respects both.

osapiens publicly describes its HUB as a multi-tenant enterprise platform with 26 modules across seven solution suites. The following are architectural risks to plan for — not claims about weaknesses of the existing platform.

Pain / Fragmentation

Capability drift across products

Different products can evolve duplicative extraction, retrieval and agent implementations with inconsistent contracts.

Gain → Shared capability contracts and rollout discipline.
Pain / Trust

Compliance answers without proof

High-stakes workflows cannot rely on plausible text alone. Each relevant assertion must trace to current, permitted evidence.

Gain → Evidence-first workflows and human review.
Pain / Economics

Costs and quality at scale

A successful demo is not proof of stable performance, fair tenant budgets or predictable unit economics in production.

Gain → Evaluation, metering, SLOs and rollback gates.

Architecture decisions

Trust boundaryTenant claims are derived from verified auth context, never trusted from a client-supplied tenantId.
Replay safetyIdempotency keys prevent duplicate downstream writes.
State modelqueued → running → needs_review / completed / failed.
PII handlingMinimize and redact logs; set retention by data classification.
Failure pathsTyped errors, bounded retries, dead-letter queue and human escalation.
CompatibilitySchema versioning, contract tests, deprecation window.
