01 / Opportunity

A real business problem before an AI decision.

DISCOVERY

Document-heavy operations

High-volume requests enter via mail and attachments; staff reconcile with CRM, ERP and policy documents. Data quality and unclear ownership stall automation.

DESIGN

Not every step needs an agent

Deterministic rules handle IDs and thresholds. LLMs help classify ambiguous documents. Humans authorize financially material and low-confidence decisions.

DELIVERY

Quality must survive go-live

Versioned decisions, observability, idempotent retries, rollback and trained owners convert a promising pilot into an operable system.

Acceptance criteria

No autonomous write on missing evidence, inconsistent identity, low confidence, high financial impact or connector outage. Every decision must return a reason, action and owner.
